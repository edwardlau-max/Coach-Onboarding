Weekly plan

Teach every skill with the 5-Steps:

Explain→ Show→ Land drill→ Practise with help→ Practise alone
Week 1

Foundations + Baby theory

Lesson 1

  • Mission, Vision, 5 Core Values
  • Child-led: never force a skill
  • 6-Stage Learn to Swim Mastery
  • 5-Steps Method; go back a step when a child struggles
  • Baby Foundation theory + videos

Lesson 2

  • Baby Beginner → Intermediate theory + videos (up to Short Distance Swim)
  • Baby lesson plan and level assessment
  • Why babies cry: cold, hungry, tired, scared of stranger
  • Task: match 10 baby skills to their stage
Week 2

Baby role play (with doll)

Lesson 1

  • Introduction script
  • Cuddle Kick, Prone Side Kick, Seated Kick, Paddling, Gripping, Cradle Back Float
  • Conditioning: "[Name], ready, go!", pour about 1 second, don't wipe face, never with a crying baby
  • Parent homework: Conditioning 6 times every bath
  • Always wear goggles, check underwater
  • Task: teach Conditioning to a "parent", word-perfect

Lesson 2

  • 1 Sec Submersion, Submerge & Glide, Fall Forward Entry
  • Free Float to Self, Free Float Between Adults
  • How to demo submersion with a real baby
  • Task: mentor checks hand position and trigger words
Week 3

Baby Beginner + Intermediate, Toddler Stage 1–4

Lesson 1 · Baby, with doll

  • Long Gliding → Assisted Swim Float Swim → Assisted Two Plane Swim → Short Distance Swim
  • Safety: Hold on Wall, Vertical Submersion (shallow, deep), Turnarounds
  • Back float up to 2 Points
  • Task: show each skill with words for the parent

Lesson 2 · Toddler

  • Kicking on Ledge, Monkey, Crocodile, Exit the Pool
  • Noodle Kicking, Self Conditioning
  • Vertical Submersion, Submerge & Glide
  • Free Float Between Adults, Free Float to Ledge (same idea as Baby Short Distance Swim)
  • Games briefing
  • Never leave a child alone on the pool edge
  • Task: teach 2 staff "parents"; toys and noodles ready first
Week 4

Group class + Baby trial lesson

Lesson 1

  • Practise with 5 staff as "parent pairs"
  • More than 4 pairs = circuit
  • Stand in one strong position; pairs pass you
  • Teach through the parent: show → parent tries → you correct
  • Visit every pair
  • Set out equipment before class

Lesson 2

  • Full Baby trial lesson practice, start to end
  • Parent FAQs, including new makeup policy
  • Task: mentor scores the trial lesson
Week 5

Baby Advanced + Toddler Beginner, Toddler Stage 5–6

Lesson 1 · Teach the pairs together

  • Baby Two Plane Swim + Turn Around ↔ Toddler Two Plane Swim
  • Baby Swim Float Swim ↔ Toddler Swim Float Swim
  • Baby Rocket ↔ Toddler Streamline Free Float, Plunge
  • Baby back floats ↔ Toddler 2 Points → Independent Back Float
  • Baby only: Lift to Breathe, Swim Around, Swim to High Wall, Exit from High Wall, Deep Diving
  • Toddler who is confident in Free Float / Short Distance Swim can learn Lift to Breathe or Swim Float Swim
  • Baby way = trigger words + parent lets go; Toddler way = self-conditioning + coach cue

Lesson 2 · Toddler

  • Face-in Kicking, One Hand Kicking, 6 Kick Change Hand
  • Rainbow Hand, Side Breathing
  • Back Kicking → Independent Backstroke
  • Crying toddler role play: comfort first, skill later, never say "stop crying"
  • Homework: own freestyle and backstroke drills
Attachment: teach 1 module, senior coach as backup.
Week 6

Revision + exam

  • Lesson 1: revise weak areas
  • Lesson 2: exam (see Assessment)
Week 7–8

Internship

  • Trial lessons first
  • Then co-teach a new class
  • First 2 solo classes checked on CCTV
  • Pass = sign-off. Not yet = more co-teaching, then check again

Assessment

To pass, the coach must pass all 3 checks.

1. Quiz (Week 6) — pass 80%

  • Real class situations, not memory questions
  • Example: "The child keeps crying, what do you do?" — coach must first ask: Baby or Toddler?
  • Which stage is this skill? Which step do you go back to?
  • Level assessment goals and makeup policy

2. Practical (Week 2–6)

  • Conditioning, word-perfect
  • Baby Foundation → Advanced skills on doll, with words for the parent
  • Submersion demo with goggles + underwater check
  • Run a 5-pair circuit, teaching through every parent
  • Baby trial lesson
  • Toddler Stage 1–6 skills with 5-Steps
  • Crying child role play
  • Own freestyle and backstroke drills

3. CCTV check (Week 7–8) — first 2 solo classes

  • Each class 3.5/5 or more
  • No score of 1
  • Score: 5 Excellent · 4 Good · 3 OK · 2 Below standard · 1 Poor
